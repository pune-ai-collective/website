College Outreach Program

Bring Industry-Leading AI Expertise to Your Campus

Partner with Pune AI Collective

Pune AI Collective invites colleges and educational institutions to collaborate with us for tech meetups and AI workshops on your campus. Our senior industry professionals bring real-world expertise and insights to inspire and educate the next generation of AI talent.

Strategic Benefits for Your Institution

This collaboration is designed to yield significant and tangible benefits for your institution, faculty, and students, aligning perfectly with goals for academic excellence and industry relevance.

A. Enhanced Student Employability and Skills

Real-World Context: Students gain direct exposure to technologies and methodologies currently used in major tech companies, enhancing their practical skill set beyond the standard curriculum.

Networking Opportunities: Direct interaction with senior industry professionals offers valuable mentorship and future career networking pathways.

B. Faculty Development and Research Alignment

Industry Insights: Faculty members can gain valuable perspectives on the latest industry challenges and research areas, helping to align course curriculum and research projects with current market demands.

Collaborative Research: The event can serve as a starting point for potential collaborative projects between your research faculty and our industry network.

C. Institutional Visibility and Brand Building

Innovation Branding: Co-hosting this event reinforces the college's image as a forward-thinking institution actively fostering innovation in emerging technologies.

Joint Publicity: The college's name and logo will be featured prominently in all promotional materials distributed across the Pune AI Collective's professional network, increasing regional visibility.

Collaboration and Resource Allocation

We propose a clear division of responsibilities to ensure the event's seamless success:

Area College Responsibilities Pune AI Collective Responsibilities
Logistics
  • Provision of a suitable lecture hall/auditorium
  • Basic IT and AV support (projector, sound system)
  • Internal student coordination and communication
  • Overall event planning and agenda setting
  • On-site coordination and time management
Speakers & Content
  • Facilitating a brief faculty/HOD welcome or introductory address
  • Securing, coordinating, and managing all senior industry speakers
  • Designing high-quality session content
Promotion
  • Internal promotion to relevant departments and student groups
  • External marketing via the PAIC professional network, social media, and industry channels
  • Creation of all joint promotional graphics
Finance
  • Just refreshments - tea/coffee costs
  • None
