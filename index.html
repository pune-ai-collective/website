Discover Our Mission

Our Mission: Empowering Pune's AI Community

To empower Pune's AI community by facilitating knowledge sharing, collaborative projects, and skill development through accessible events and resources, enabling members to innovate, grow professionally, and apply AI solutions that address local and global needs.

Our Vision: Pune as a Global AI Innovation Hub

To establish Pune as a globally recognized epicenter of AI innovation, where a thriving ecosystem of diverse talents propels groundbreaking advancements, inspires the next generation of AI leaders, and positions the city at the forefront of technological transformation.

Our Meetups: Regular Knowledge-Sharing Events

We organize regular meetups to bring together AI enthusiasts, researchers, and professionals in Pune. Join us to learn, network, and collaborate on cutting-edge AI projects and innovations.

Oxford-Style Debate

February 4th, 2026

First Oxford-style debate tackling the motion: "Should we ban GenAI use in production code reviews?"

Teams:

Team "Ban" (Kapil Nema, Sushilkumar Deshmukh, Aniket Paluskar): Focused on trust and security risks from AI hallucinations.

Team "Build" (Jigar Raisinghani, Rigin Oommen, Deep Gandhi): Argued AI supercharges development with proper human oversight.

Verdict: Majority voted Against the Ban (Pre: 60 vs 31, Post: 35 vs 23)
